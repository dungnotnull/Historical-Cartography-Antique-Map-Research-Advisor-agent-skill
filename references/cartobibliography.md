# Reference — Cartobibliographic Methodology (State/Edition/Issue)

> Distilled from Tooley (1978) *Dictionary of Mapmakers*; Koeman (1967–71) / van der Krogt (1997–2011) *Atlantes Neerlandici*; Karrow (1993); Goss (1990). Operationalised by the `cartobibliographic_lookup` tool and the `CartobibliographyAdvisor`.

## Core principle
Cartobibliography identifies a map's **state, edition, and issue** by tracking plate changes, title-page variants, map-count, and plate-acquisition history. A *state* = a single plate altered between printings; an *edition* = a discrete published set; an *issue* = a re-issue of sheets under a new title/imprint.

## State/edition identification checklist
1. **Read the imprint** (publisher, place, date) on both map and title-page.
2. **Count the maps** in the atlas — many atlases grow edition by edition (e.g. Ortelius *Theatrum*: 53 maps in 1570, expanding in later editions).
3. **Identify the title-page** variant — title-pages are re-engraved between editions and are strong edition markers.
4. **Trace plate-acquisition history**: Mercator → Hondius (c.1604); Hondius → Blaeu (c.1629); these transfers produce re-issues with new imprints.
5. **Cartouche signatures** distinguish near-identical rivals (e.g. Janssonius mimicking Blaeu).
6. **Record plate alterations** (added place-names, changed coastlines, privilege dates) → state sequence.

## Plate-acquisition chain (Dutch golden age, key cases)
- Mercator (1595 posthumous atlas) → plates sold to **Jodocus Hondius** c.1604 → **Mercator-Hondius** editions 1606+.
- Hondius plates → passed to **Jan Janssonius**.
- Hondius plates bought by **Willem Blaeu** c.1629 → **Joan Blaeu** expands to *Atlas Maior* (1662–65); firm declines after 1672 fire.

## Imprint → period quick rules
- "J.B. Homann" imprint → c.1702–1730; "Homann Heirs" → post-1730.
- "Blaeu" + *Atlas Maior* → 1662–1665 window.
- *Theatrum Orbis Terrarum* + Ortelius imprint → 1570 onward; map-count identifies edition.
- "Mercator-Hondius" on title-page → 1606 onward (not the 1595 Mercator original).

## Guardrail
Cartobibliography supports **attribution and edition identification**, not certified authentication. Refer to an accredited specialist for valuation.
