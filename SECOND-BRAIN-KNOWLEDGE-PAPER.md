## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. R. Tooley (1978). Tooley's Dictionary of Mapmakers. Meridian Publishing — Foundational cartographer/publisher attribution reference.
2. D. Woodward (ed.) (1987-2007). The History of Cartography, Vol. 1-6. University of Chicago Press — Comprehensive authoritative history-of-cartography reference series.
3. P. van der Krogt (1997-2011). Koeman's Atlantes Neerlandici, New Edition, Vol. 1-4. HES & De Graaf Publishers — Foundational cartobibliographic methodology reference for Dutch atlases.
4. C. Koeman (1967-1971). Atlantes Neerlandici, Vol. 1-5. Theatrum Orbis Terrarum — Foundational cartobibliographic state/edition-identification methodology.
5. J. Snyder (1993). Flattening the Earth: Two Thousand Years of Map Projections. University of Chicago Press — Foundational comprehensive history-of-map-projections reference.
6. R. Shirley (1983). The Mapping of the World: Early Printed World Maps 1472-1700. Holland Press — Foundational applied printed-map dating and cataloguing reference.
7. D. Woodward (1996). Maps as Prints in the Italian Renaissance: Makers, Distributors and Consumers. British Library — Applied print-technique history reference for dating.
8. P. Barber, T. Harper (2010). Magnificent Maps: Power, Propaganda and Art. British Library — Applied historical-content and context analysis reference.
9. C. Koeman, G. Schilder, M. van Egmond, P. van der Krogt (2007). Commercial Cartography and Map Production in the Low Countries, 1500-ca. 1672. In The History of Cartography, Vol. 3 — Applied regional cartobibliographic reference.
10. E. Heawood (1932). Watermarks Mainly of the 17th and 18th Centuries. Paper Publications Society — Foundational watermark-analysis reference for paper dating and provenance research.
11. D. Smith (1986). Antique Maps of the British Isles. B.T. Batsford — Applied regional cartographic-history and dating reference.
12. R. Karrow (1993). Mapmakers of the Sixteenth Century and Their Maps. Speculum Orbis Press — Foundational biographical/attribution reference for early cartographers.
13. J. Goss (1990). Blaeu's The Grand Atlas of the 17th Century World. Studio Editions — Applied case-study reference for state/edition identification methodology.
14. M. Edney (1997). Mapping an Empire: The Geographical Construction of British India, 1765-1843. University of Chicago Press — Applied case-study reference for historical-content/boundary-history dating analysis.
15. P. Barber (ed.) (2005). The Map Book. Walker & Company — Comprehensive applied historical-map reference spanning major periods and techniques.
16. D. Woodward (1975). Five Centuries of Map Printing. University of Chicago Press — Foundational print-technique chronology reference.
17. C. Verner (1965). Copperplate Printing. In Five Centuries of Map Printing — Foundational engraving-technique dating reference.
18. International Map Collectors' Society (2020). Guidelines on Map Authentication and Valuation. IMCoS — Applied professional standard relevant to accredited-appraiser referral.
19. R. Pflederer (2009). Census of Ptolemy's Geography. American Philosophical Society — Applied cartobibliographic census methodology for early cartographic works.
20. T. Campbell (1987). Early Maps. Abbeville Press — Standard applied introductory historical-cartography reference covering dating and authentication basics.
